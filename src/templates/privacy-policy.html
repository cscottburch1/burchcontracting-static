Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Burch Contracting ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information you submit through burchcontracting.com (the "Site").

Information We Collect

When you submit our contact or consultation request form, we collect the information you provide directly, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Street address, city, state, and zip code
  • Details about your project (type, budget range, timeframe, and description)
  • Any photos, plans, or documents you choose to upload as attachments

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit solely to respond to your inquiry, prepare a consultation or estimate, and communicate with you about your project. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

reCAPTCHA

Our contact form is protected by Google reCAPTCHA to help prevent spam and abuse. Use of reCAPTCHA is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Data Retention & Security

We retain inquiry information only as long as needed to respond to your request and maintain reasonable business records. We take reasonable technical and administrative precautions to protect the information you submit, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Contact Us
